How: Measuring Resonances

Made for CoreXY printers

On a CoreXY printer both belts pull the toolhead, so they must match. This printer's kinematics: .

How it works
  1. Set the target frequency your printer's guide gives.
  2. Move the toolhead where the guide says (the pad above), and save that spot for next time.
  3. Tap the toolhead once: Chordata learns how it rings by itself, so a knock is never taken for a belt.
  4. Pluck belt A about once a second until 5 good plucks are counted, then belt B.
  5. Turn one tensioner a little as advised and measure both again, until both are on target.

Which belt you call A doesn't matter: the goal is equal tension. Chordata only moves the toolhead when you press a button.

Pluck measurements

Frequency is measured in Hz. Force requires belt mass and span length. Use your printer manufacturer's target and measurement position.
